How It Began

A Rite of Passage That Became a Pact

Every great tradition starts with a milestone. Bourbon 10 was born out of a simple, unforgettable occasion: celebrating the 21st birthday of one of our own. What started as a rite of passage evolved into a lifelong pact.

We realized that as time moves on, brotherhood shouldn't just be maintained — it should be celebrated. Every pour is a promise. Every bottle opened marks a chapter we chose to write together.
