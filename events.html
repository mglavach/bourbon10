The Plan

Tentative Distillery Schedule

Welcome everybody — should be a great weekend in November celebrating! Right now the tentative schedule has us hitting three distinct days of distilleries. More details to follow as dates lock in.

01
First Day
Craft, Character & Bob Dylan's Bar
NDP • Independent Bottler
Pursuit Spirits
A non-distiller producer — the kind of place that hunts single barrels and blends them into something worth talking about. We've been doing research and this one looks great.
Distillery
Rabbit Hole Distillery
Louisville's art-forward distillery. Five-story glass atrium, open-air rickhouse views, and bourbon that earns its shelf space.
Bar • Must-Do
The Last Refuge
Bob Dylan's bourbon bar housed in a converted old Methodist church. Willett Purple Top for $14 a pour last time we checked. The pictures alone should sell you on this one.
02
Second Day
The Legends, a Comeback & a Private Pour
Distillery • Icon
Buffalo Trace
The Mount Rushmore of American whiskey. Blanton's, Eagle Rare, Pappy — it all starts here. Non-negotiable on any Kentucky trip.
Distillery • Redemption
Whiskey Thief
Redemption for Uncle Dan. Whatever happened last time — this is where we make it right.
Distillery • Private Access
Castle & Key
We have a family friend with barrels housed here. He's pulling strings so we can taste directly from those barrels. This one is not on the public tour.
03
Third Day
Bardstown, History & the House of Willett
Distillery
Bardstown Bourbon Company
One of the most celebrated newer distilleries in the state. Collaborative distilling model, spectacular campus, restaurant on-site.
Distillery • Heritage
Heaven Hill
America's largest independent, family-owned spirits company. Elijah Craig, Evan Williams, Larceny — the breadth alone is worth the stop.
Distillery • The Closer
Willett Distillery
We end on the hill. Pot Still Reserve, Family Estate, the Purple Top that started conversations at The Last Refuge. A fitting way to close out the weekend.
